ABOUT THE CAMP

Healthcare that comes closer to you.

Sehat Care is a community-focused health camp designed to make basic screenings and professional guidance more accessible.

Our team helps visitors understand common health measurements and connect with healthcare professionals. The camp is intended for general screening and does not replace emergency medical care.
